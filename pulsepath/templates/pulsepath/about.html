our mission

PulsePath is redefining how festivals manage crowds and safety. With real-time data, custom alerts, and interactive mapping, we help organisers and attendees experience festivals that are not just smarter — but safer, smoother, and more unforgettable.

live map tracking

Festival organisers can view real-time foot traffic with PulsePath’s interactive map — enabling smarter decisions on the fly.

capacity awareness

Dynamic icons alert when facilities are at low, medium, or high occupancy — including toilets, stages, and vendor zones.

smart notifications

Both organisers and attendees receive nudges when crowd flow changes — helping reduce bottlenecks and improve safety.

peace of mind

No more manual counting or guessing. PulsePath gives organisers control and festival-goers the freedom to roam confidently.
